Free RRB NTPC Maths PYQ chapter-wise online tests: 26 chapters, 50-question sets, 40-minute timer, negative marking and answer review.

How the RRB NTPC Maths PYQ tests work

Pick a chapter, then pick a set. Every set has up to 50 previous-year questions from RRB NTPC CBT-1 (Undergraduate and Graduate, 2024 and 2025). You get 40 minutes, +1 for a right answer and −1/3 for a wrong one. After you submit, you can review every question with the correct answer. Your progress is saved on your own phone or computer.

Pair these tests with the RRB NTPC 30-Day PYQ Revision Tracker and the RRB NTPC 60-Day Study Plan.
